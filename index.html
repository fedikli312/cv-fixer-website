Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

CV Fixer ("the app", "we", "us") helps you score and improve your CV. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, which service providers process data on our behalf, and the choices available to you.

What we collect

  • Account data — your email address and account identifier, handled by Supabase when you sign in.
  • Your CV — the file you upload and extracted text, used for CV Fixer features.
  • Job descriptions — job posting text you paste.
  • Subscription and purchase information — managed through the App Store and RevenueCat.
  • Product interaction data — events such as app launch, CV upload, score viewed, paywall viewed, trial start and purchase completion.
  • Device identifiers — advertising/device identifiers may be available where permitted by settings and ATT.

How your CV is processed

To generate AI feedback, improved CVs and cover letters, CV text and job descriptions may be sent to OpenAI to return the result. Your CV is not used by CV Fixer to train AI models, and is never published or sold.

Analytics and advertising measurement

We use Meta App Events and the TikTok App Events SDK to understand product interactions and measure CV Fixer's own marketing. These services may receive limited event and attribution information where permitted.

We do not send your CV text, uploaded CV file contents, job descriptions, email address, phone number, or other CV content to Meta or TikTok through our analytics event integration.

App Tracking Transparency and advertising identifiers

On iOS, CV Fixer uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before enabling access to advertising identifiers for tracking. If you allow tracking, Meta and TikTok may use permitted device identifiers and product interaction data for attribution and measurement. If you deny tracking, advertising tracking remains disabled.

Service providers

We use Supabase, RevenueCat, OpenAI, Meta and TikTok to operate relevant parts of the service.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data or your CV.
  • We never share your CV publicly.
  • We never send CV text or job-description content to Meta or TikTok through analytics events.
  • We never show third-party advertising in the app.

Your choices and rights

You can sign out at any time from Settings. You can choose whether to allow iOS tracking through Apple's ATT prompt. To request access to, or deletion of, your account and associated data, contact us below. If you are in Türkiye, you have rights under KVKK; if you are in the EU/UK, you have rights under GDPR.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the last updated date.
